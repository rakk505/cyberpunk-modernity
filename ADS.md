# Animated Advertising Displays

Advertising comes in three deliberately separate forms. Manual wall placement uses an `8x4`
panel. Generated Arnis facades remain large: the offline scan catalogs the largest eligible
rectangle on every disconnected exterior face component, from 8-16 blocks wide and 4-256 blocks
high. The current catalog contains 3,744 surfaces across 2,420 of 17,920 audited templates,
including 3,561 tile-boundary facades. It never attaches the small street format to a building.

Every district's central generation chunk also searches for open, flat space for two solid
freestanding structures. Medium ads are exactly `2x6x8` and show the animated clip across both
`6x8` faces. Small ads are exactly `2x2x4`, show a synchronized card on all four `2x4` faces, and
silently rotate only between the original Meta, ClosedAI, and MisAnthropic parody cards.

`tools/advertising/find_large_ad_surfaces.py` reads the audited structure NBTs, finds exposed solid
wall components, prefers rectangles containing sea lanterns, glowstone, or another full light
block, rejects any candidate rectangle containing glass, and emits deterministic placements.
Runtime generation performs only a catalog lookup and final world-state validation. Boundary
candidates wait until every affected city chunk is final, then require the whole face, a
three-block front volume, and a bounded outward ray to remain open. Catalog v3 stores compact
support hashes so backfill can reject player-modified facades without shipping millions of block
names. Generated facades use one rendering anchor rather than a panel-block grid, preserving the
wall and keeping tall displays cheap. Existing generated chunks are migrated one loaded chunk per
tick; backfill skips fixed or recovered mainline buildings and retries unloaded neighbors on a
bounded cooldown. Generated overlays fill the selected facade and render up to 256 blocks away.

Building campaigns follow authoritative atlas ownership: District M uses the four Meta animations,
District O uses ClosedAI, and every other district uses the general rotation containing the three
original campaigns, Misanthropic, and all four Meta animations. The campaign id is stored on the
display anchor, so reloads and boundary-tile transforms do not change the selected advertiser.

Buildings in the first row along an inter-district highway carry a separate, live-scanned
megascreen. `HighwayFacadeAdGeneration` picks the one wall in the chunk that actually faces the
connection, finds the largest window-free rectangle on a single coplanar plane of that wall, and
mounts one overlay there. Those screens always use the dedicated `highway` campaign (`vater`,
`gojo`, `horizon`, `meta_logo_2`, `petrochem`) rather than the district rotation, so a corridor
reads as one continuous run of roadside advertising. A chunk that already anchors a generated
display on the highway-facing wall is left alone, and reserved story/gig building chunks are
skipped entirely. Progress is persisted per chunk, so existing saves backfill their highway
facades once and never rescan.

The five `highway` clips are third-party recordings supplied by the project owner as source links;
see `ASSET_SOURCES.md` for their links and hashes. They are silent, looped or trimmed to 30
seconds, and aspect-fitted against a black pad.

The three original campaigns are generated by `tools/create_demo_ads.py`. The four Meta campaigns
use real Meta advertisement videos (`meta_logo`, `meta_glasses`, `meta_ai`, `meta_future`) whose
inclusion was confirmed by the project owner; their original source links and hashes remain in
`ASSET_SOURCES.md`. The project-supplied ClosedAI and Misanthropic recordings are short silent
sources that are looped, aspect-fitted, and hash-checked. All clips last 30-45 seconds. Minecraft
does not decode video natively, so runtime assets are preprocessed to shared 4x4 sprite sheets at
160x90. Every campaign runs at 8 FPS. Only the three original campaigns have spatial audio.

```bash
python3 tools/create_demo_ads.py --ffmpeg /path/to/ffmpeg
python3 tools/generate_meta_ads.py
python3 tools/process_ads.py --ffmpeg /path/to/ffmpeg
python3 tools/generate_ad_logos.py
python3 tools/advertising/find_large_ad_surfaces.py
```

`process_ads.py` validates MP4/MOV metadata, duration, identifiers, source hashes, campaign ids,
audio policy, total frame count, and the maximum sheet budget before atomically replacing assets.
